CrickeTrack

CrickeTrack is an internal ad-reporting tool operated by Stenvel AB (org. no. 559590-8533). It is used exclusively by our own team to pull advertising performance statistics for Stenvel AB's own brands from Google Ads and compile them into internal reports.

The tool is not offered to the public and is only used with Google accounts belonging to Stenvel AB.
