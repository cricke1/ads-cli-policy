CrickeTrack – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

CrickeTrack is an internal ad-reporting tool operated by Stenvel AB (org. no. 559590-8533), used only by our own team for Stenvel AB's own brands.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Ads performance statistics (campaigns, ad groups, ads, spend, impressions, clicks, conversions) for Google Ads accounts owned by Stenvel AB, accessed through Google OAuth with the account owner's consent.
  • Basic Google account identifiers needed to complete the OAuth sign-in.

How data is used and stored

Data is used solely to generate internal advertising reports. It is stored locally on computers owned and controlled by Stenvel AB. OAuth tokens are stored locally and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing

CrickeTrack does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. It does not collect personal data about consumers.

Google API Services User Data Policy

CrickeTrack's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data deletion

To have data deleted, contact us at the address below and it will be deleted within 30 days.
