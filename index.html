Ads CLI – Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Ads CLI is an internal advertising-management tool operated by Nordic Patches (nordicpatches.com). It is used exclusively by our own team to manage and analyze our own Meta (Facebook/Instagram) advertising accounts via the Meta Marketing API.

Data the app accesses

  • Advertising performance data (campaigns, ad sets, ads, spend, impressions, clicks, conversions) for ad accounts owned by Nordic Patches.
  • Content and engagement data (comments, messages) on our own Facebook Page and ads.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store, or process personal data from consumers or third parties.
  • It has no public-facing login and is not available to anyone outside our organization.
  • It does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.

Data storage

Aggregated advertising metrics are stored on systems owned and controlled by Nordic Patches, used solely for internal reporting and ad management.

Data deletion

The app does not hold personal data about app users or consumers. If you believe data about you is held via this app and wish to have it deleted, contact us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days.
